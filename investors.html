For Investors & Strategic Partners

Investment origination and advisory for DRC mining

Kephas starts with the investor's mandate. We identify opportunities that fit its criteria and work with Congolese mining counsel to assess the rights, counterparties, and records behind each opportunity.

Kephas acts as an independent originator and transaction adviser.

What the investment team receives

An initial screening brief describes the proposed transaction, identifies the records Kephas holds, and distinguishes what counsel has confirmed from what qualified independent advisers have verified. Where material questions remain, the brief sets out the work required, who should perform it, and the decision it would inform.

We can help you:

  • Identify opportunities that fit your mandate
  • Coordinate title and counterparty diligence
  • Identify diligence requirements
  • Assess transaction pathways
  • Coordinate qualified local engagement
