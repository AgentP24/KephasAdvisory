What we do

We do the work that comes before the deal.

In the Democratic Republic of Congo, deals are lost to bad information more than anything else. We surface the problem in a title or a counterparty before your capital is committed.

  • 01Verify the title. We pull the permit from the official cadastre, reconstruct the chain of title, and flag any gap, lapse, or competing claim.
  • 02Screen the counterparty. We trace beneficial ownership to the individual and screen for sanctions and political exposure.
  • 03Structure the entry. We lay out the lawful routes at your ticket size, what each costs to test, and which to rule out.
  • 04Put it in writing. Every engagement ends in a document you can take to counsel or your board, with verified claims and sources.
How we work

Advisors, not brokers.

We work for a fixed fee, set before we start. We take no percentage of the deal and no position in the asset.

No stake in the outcome

No fee we earn depends on a permit, an approval, or any government decision.

Sourced and dated

We grade every finding by the strength of its source and keep the evidence on file. You act on the record, not on representation.

Built for compliance review

FCPA and UK Bribery Act aligned, with sanctions and PEP screening on every counterparty.

Starting is simple

A call, then a memo.

Start with a short call, no charge. Whether you're weighing a single asset or planning your entry into the DRC, tell us where you are. If we can help, the first engagement is a fixed-fee scoping memo, usually two to four weeks. When the fit is right, we also bring opportunities we've already begun to verify.
