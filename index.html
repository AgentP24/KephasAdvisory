From local opportunity to institutional decision.

The Democratic Republic of Congo presents significant opportunities across critical minerals, mining, infrastructure, and related supply chains.

But opportunity alone is not enough.

For international capital, the critical questions are often more fundamental:

  • Who owns the asset?
  • Is the title valid?
  • What exactly is being offered?
  • Who are the counterparties?
  • What regulatory requirements apply?
  • What risks remain unresolved?
  • What would it take to make the opportunity investment-ready?

Kephas helps answer those questions before they become expensive problems.

What We Do

Verify. Assess. De-risk. Structure.

Verify.

Asset & Title Verification

We help clients establish what an asset actually is before evaluating what it could become.

Our work can include:

  • Mining title and permit review
  • Ownership and chain-of-title analysis
  • Corporate and beneficial ownership screening
  • Counterparty verification
  • Documentation review
  • Regulatory status assessment
  • Identification of inconsistencies and information gaps

The objective: establish a reliable factual foundation for further diligence.

Assess.

Market & Investment Intelligence

A project can possess valuable mineralization and still be commercially unsuitable.

We assess the factors that influence whether an opportunity merits deeper consideration, including:

  • Mineral and commodity exposure
  • Project stage
  • Infrastructure
  • Logistics
  • Regulatory environment
  • Local operating considerations
  • Counterparty quality
  • Capital requirements
  • Potential transaction structures
  • Key diligence gaps

The objective: distinguish an interesting opportunity from an actionable one.

De-risk.

Investment Readiness

Kephas helps identify what needs to be resolved before an opportunity can be presented to sophisticated capital.

We organize the information necessary for investors, strategic partners, counsel, and decision-makers to conduct meaningful diligence.

Where gaps exist, we help define the work required to close them.

The objective: move from fragmented information to an investment-ready process.

Structure.

Transaction Advisory

Different assets require different paths to commercialization.

Kephas helps clients evaluate potential routes including:

  • Strategic partnerships
  • Joint ventures
  • Project-level investment
  • Acquisition structures
  • Farm-ins and earn-ins
  • Offtake relationships
  • Development partnerships
  • Capital introductions
  • Other appropriate commercial structures

Our role is to help clients understand the transaction landscape and prepare for informed engagement.

Specialized Advisory

PR → PE Readiness

For holders of exploration-stage mining rights, the path from exploration to commercial development can involve significant technical, regulatory, corporate, and capital considerations.

Kephas can help assess and organize that pathway.

Our work may include:

  • 01Title & ownership review. Establish the current legal and corporate position.
  • 02Geological information assessment. Identify the available technical evidence and material gaps.
  • 03Exploration & development planning. Help define the information necessary to advance the opportunity.
  • 04Regulatory pathway assessment. Evaluate applicable permitting and development requirements.
  • 05Investment-readiness preparation. Organize the opportunity for institutional diligence.
  • 06Strategic partner identification. Determine what type of capital or operating partner may be appropriate.
  • 07Transaction preparation. Support the process from qualified interest through diligence and commercial negotiation.

The objective: transform an exploration opportunity into an opportunity that sophisticated capital can evaluate.

Kephas does not guarantee regulatory approvals, permit conversions, financing, investment returns, or transaction completion.

Responsible Mineral Supply Chains

Formalization. Traceability. Responsible Sourcing.

Artisanal and small-scale mining can represent both significant opportunity and significant diligence complexity.

Kephas evaluates opportunities with attention to:

  • Legal and regulatory status
  • Responsible sourcing
  • Traceability
  • Supply-chain transparency
  • Counterparty integrity
  • Formalization pathways
  • Community considerations
  • Applicable international standards

The objective is not simply to identify mineral supply.

It is to understand where that supply comes from, who controls it, and whether it can support a credible commercial chain.

How We Work
