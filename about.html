About Kephas

We help international capital understand the DRC.

Kephas Advisory was built around a simple observation:

The largest risk in an emerging-market transaction is often not the opportunity itself. It is what the buyer does not know.

Our work is designed to reduce that information gap.

We combine DRC market intelligence with an institutional approach to verification, diligence, and transaction preparation.

We do not simply introduce parties.

We help clients understand what they are evaluating before they decide what to do with it.

Our Philosophy

Local knowledge without institutional discipline is insufficient.

Institutional capital without local context is exposed.

The intersection is where Kephas operates.
