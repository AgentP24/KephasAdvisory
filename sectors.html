Opportunity Intelligence

DRC Critical Minerals

Kephas maintains a focus on opportunities across the DRC's mineral economy, including:

Copper

Major industrial and strategic demand with established infrastructure and supply-chain relevance.

Cobalt

A strategically important battery mineral with significant DRC production and increasingly sophisticated responsible-sourcing requirements.

Tantalum

Critical to electronics and advanced manufacturing supply chains.

Tin & Tungsten

Important industrial and technology minerals with established global demand.

Lithium & Niobium

Emerging strategic areas requiring careful project-level evaluation.

Gold & Diamonds

Established sectors with distinct regulatory, operational, and supply-chain considerations.

Other Strategic Minerals

We evaluate opportunities selectively based on asset quality, legal position, commercial potential, infrastructure, counterparties, and investor fit.
